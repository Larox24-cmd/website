U4 · Research

SDCF: a bus with no master

Self-Configuring Deterministic Control Fabric — independent research since August 2024 on a masterless, fault-tolerant bus protocol for deterministic industrial control networks.

PIC18F57Q83 nodes on a shared RS-485 bus allocate collision-free TDMA slots among themselves and absorb peer failures with no coordinator node.

63nodes converge within 88 frames, worst case
3frames for peer takeover, against an analytical bound of 4
7,200words in the technical manuscript, in preparation

Characterized in a Python simulator, then ported to JavaScript with a browser visualizer for schedule inspection. A reference firmware implementation is underway in MPLAB X, and I measured fault-recovery bounds against the analytical model.

  • TDMA
  • Deterministic scheduling
